Stop secrets, PII, and source code from leaking into AI tools.

**Summary (≤132 chars, matches the Chrome listing):**
Stops secrets, PII, and proprietary data from being pasted into AI tools — scans prompts and warns or blocks before they're sent.

**Summary (≤256 chars):**
Palivane is the AI security gateway for the whole org: browser extension, coding-tool hooks, LLM gateway, egress proxy, SaaS + Teams/Slack scanning, and CI pipelines — one console showing which AI tools are in use and what sensitive data reached them.

**Long description:**
> Your teams already use AI everywhere: ChatGPT and Claude in the browser, Copilot and
> Cursor in editors, agents in CI, and AI integrations reading Slack, Teams, Drive, and
> SharePoint. Palivane covers every one of those surfaces with a single deterministic
> detection engine — 500+ AI destinations cataloged, ~45 detection checks, ~55 credential
> formats — and one findings console.
>
> - **Prevent**: block, redact, or tokenize secrets/PII/source code in prompts before
>   they reach a model (sub-millisecond median scoring). Confirmed credentials are
>   hard-stopped even in monitor mode.
> - **Discover**: shadow-AI inventory across browsers, CLIs, OAuth grants, and CI —
>   including the devices reporting nothing, named rather than counted as clean.
> - **Govern**: agent least-privilege (MCP tool-call checks before execution),
>   justified-proceed workflows, SCIM lifecycle, SIEM/S3 export, EU AI Act mapping.
> - **Deploy**: one command per machine or an MDM pack (Jamf/Intune/GPO) for the fleet;
>   fully self-hostable — the same engine ships as a container.
>
> SOC 2 program underway; DPA in-product; deterministic (no AI-in-the-loop) detection by
> default with an optional BYOK LLM judge.

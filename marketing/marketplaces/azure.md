# Azure Marketplace — "Contact me" listing (zero integration)

Fastest of the three: a lead-gen listing that transacts nothing and needs no fulfillment
code. Leads land in Partner Center (connect the CRM later or poll the leads UI).

1. partner.microsoft.com → join **Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program** (free) with the
   SOC Foundry work account; verify the business (may take a couple of days).
2. Partner Center → **Marketplace offers** → New offer → **Software as a Service**.
3. Setup: "Get it now? → **Contact me**" (this is the zero-integration path).
4. Offer listing: paste from `listing-copy.md`; logos from `assets/` (48/90/216 required);
   screenshots: `extension/store-assets/block-1280x800.png` + console shots (1280×720 min).
5. Preview audience → submit for certification (content-only review, typically days).

Upgrade path later: same offer converts to a transactable SaaS plan by adding the
SaaS Fulfillment v2 + landing-page integration.
